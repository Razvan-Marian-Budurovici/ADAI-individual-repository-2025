Q: how should the dataset be spit into training/validation/test data ? For example per year?

A: ideally to have seasonality each month would be a datapoint, but with so little training data, solutions would include: 
- bundling months from the same season together (instead of predicting for December you predict for a month in the winter) where each season could have 9 months of data (3 years * 3 months per season) so we could have 5 months * 4 seasons (datapoints) for training 2 * 4 for validation and 2 * 4 for testing (20 + 8 + 8), this would need the month to be labeled for their season
- we could forgo seasonality due to lack of data, then out of 36 months we use 18 for training 9 for validation and 9 for testing
- another option could be to generate some mock data to train the predictive model to solve the lack of data issue, but this is unreliable and creates unrealistic results
